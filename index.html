Chrome Extension · Web App

EarthTime

A beautiful, living world day & night clock. Open a tab and see exactly where it's day and where it's night, right now — a real-time sun terminator over a flat map or a spinnable 3D globe under a live night sky.

Get it on the Chrome Web Store Open the web app →

Private by default · no sign-in · no tracking — your pins & settings stay on your device.

No install needed: the web app runs the whole clock right in your browser. Its pins are saved on your device, separate from the extension's.

What it does

Real-time day & night

The exact solar terminator with soft twilight bands, the subsolar point, the dateline, and day-of-week tabs.

Spinnable 3D globe

Drag to spin it like a real globe; it slows gently to a stop. Scroll to zoom, or leave it to auto-rotate.

A live night sky

Thousands of stars, the Milky Way, and the naked-eye planets in their real positions for today.

World-city clocks

A configurable strip of 240+ cities, auto-sorted west → east, so you always know the time where your people are.

Family pins

Drop a pin anywhere on the map or globe, name it, and get a live local clock for that exact spot.

Hover & measure

Hover for the nearest city, local time, and sunrise/sunset. Drag to measure great-circle distance, bearing, and flight time.

Privacy: EarthTime collects nothing and sends nothing — no accounts, no tracking, no ads. Your pins & settings stay on your device. The Chrome extension bundles all its imagery and makes no network requests at all; the web app loads only its own files, then runs entirely in your browser. Read the full privacy policy.
